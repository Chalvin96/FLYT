# Learning loop

Lessons teach structured concepts, reading supplies authentic context, lexicon lookup explains words, and review schedules durable recall. Saved words flow from reading through the lexicon into learner cards; lesson completion enrolls pools without replacing existing learner state.

The public landing surface demonstrates this loop with representative reading, lookup, and review previews. It presents Flyt as a free Bokmål product with no paid tier, expiring trial, or payment card requirement.

The product favors transparent feedback, native text exposure, accessible interactions, and mature status copy. A learner should always know whether an operation is graded, pending, retryable, or unconfirmed.

Review startup distinguishes an issuance failure from an empty queue. Learners can
retry a failed initial load and only enter the completion state after a successful
queue response.
