# Proposal: First class dictionary expressions

## Why

Ordbøkene expressions are valid learner vocabulary but Flyt currently rejects their part of speech and punctuation heavy headwords at the lookup boundary.

## Scope

Add `expression` to the durable lemma POS contract and fresh schema, accept normalized dictionary punctuation through lookup and autocomplete, and render bracket alternatives readably while retaining the canonical headword. Expression variant expansion and reading phrase recognition remain out of scope.
